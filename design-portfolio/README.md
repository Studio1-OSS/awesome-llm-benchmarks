## Heuristic

| Criterion | Weight | What good looks like |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Art direction | 30% | Type, color, image treatment, and layout form a confident, specific point of view. |
| Editorial hierarchy | 25% | Work, context, and calls to action are easy to scan and naturally sequenced. |
| Interaction and motion | 20% | Movement and navigation feel intentional and never compete with the work. |
| Responsive craft | 15% | The design remains legible and persuasive on small and large screens. |
| Technical quality | 10% | The artifact is stable, accessible, and self-contained. |
